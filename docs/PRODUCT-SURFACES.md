# Product Surfaces

The MineralView platform is organized around three primary product surfaces.

Owner Intelligence  
Explore  
Advanced View

Each surface represents a different level of interaction with the platform's deterministic mineral datasets.

The surfaces are not separate applications. They operate together within a single system connected by the Continuous Intelligence Layer.

This structure allows the platform to remain accessible to mineral owners while still supporting advanced analysis for experienced users and professionals.

---

# Owner Intelligence

Owner Intelligence is the primary product surface of the platform.

It is designed for mineral owners who want to understand what is happening with their minerals without needing to interpret complex industry datasets.

Owner Intelligence focuses on explanation rather than raw data exploration.

Typical capabilities include:

- explanations of production changes
- summaries of nearby drilling activity
- interpretation of operator behavior
- contextual insights about mineral value drivers
- monitoring of events affecting the user's minerals

Evidence supporting these insights is presented through lightweight visual indicators such as:

- simplified production charts
- activity indicators
- geographic context

Each insight links to the underlying data available through Explore.

Owner Intelligence represents the default experience for most users.

---

# Explore

Explore provides direct access to the deterministic datasets used by the intelligence system.

It functions as the verification and discovery layer of the platform.

Users can inspect:

- mineral ownership records
- well production history
- drilling and completion activity
- nearby wells on geographic maps
- regulatory filings and reports

Explore allows users to validate the information presented in Owner Intelligence.

For many users, Explore becomes the next step after an intelligence insight raises curiosity about the underlying data.

Explore also allows more technically inclined mineral owners to analyze data directly.

---

# Advanced View

Advanced View provides deeper analytics tools for advanced users and professionals.

It supports more complex workflows such as:

- portfolio-level mineral analysis
- detailed production trend analysis
- operator performance comparisons
- deeper inspection of well activity
- advanced filtering of datasets

Advanced View allows the platform to support professional mineral analysis without complicating the default owner experience.

Access to Advanced View may be controlled through persona-based routing.

Typical users include:

- advanced mineral owners
- mineral buyers
- land professionals
- analysts
- operators

The three product surfaces represent increasing levels of analytical depth.


Owner Intelligence

▼

Users typically enter the platform through Owner Intelligence.

As curiosity grows, users may move into Explore to inspect the underlying data.

Advanced users may then access Advanced View for deeper analysis.

The platform does not force this progression but allows it naturally through user behavior.

---

# Continuous Intelligence Integration

The Continuous Intelligence Layer connects the three product surfaces.

Rather than isolating intelligence to a single screen, the system preserves interpretive context as users navigate through the platform.

This allows the platform to continuously answer the core question:

**What does this mean for my minerals?**

Examples include:

- interpreting production trends while viewing production charts
- explaining nearby drilling while viewing the map
- summarizing operator activity while reviewing reports

This design allows the platform to function as a unified intelligence environment rather than a collection of disconnected dashboards.

# Persona Alignment

Each product surface aligns with a different level of user sophistication.

| Persona | Primary Surface |
|-------|--------|
| Mineral Owner | Owner Intelligence |
| Advanced Owner | Explore |
| Professional User | Advanced View |

Persona-aware routing and behavioral signals may adjust the visibility of advanced features.

This ensures the platform remains approachable for mineral owners while still supporting advanced analysis.

---

# Design Principles

The three-surface model follows several design principles.

## Interpretation First

The platform prioritizes explanation before raw data.

## Data Transparency

All intelligence insights link to deterministic datasets.

## Progressive Depth

Users can gradually move from simple explanations to deeper analysis.

## Continuous Intelligence

Interpretation remains available as users navigate between product surfaces.
